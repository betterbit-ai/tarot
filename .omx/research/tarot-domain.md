# Tarot Domain Research

The canonical catalog uses stable ids 0-77, 22 Major Arcana and four 14-card suits. A canonical combination is order-independent while runtime positions preserve pick order. Authored trio stories override deterministic fallback; both return the same reading shape.

Natural Korean must describe a flow, a tension, a direction, and a practical next stance without certainty or AI language. Details and validation rules are in `docs/TAROT_SPEC.md` and `spec/spec.md`.
