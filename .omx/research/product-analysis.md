# Product Analysis

The core contract and prioritized acceptance criteria are frozen in `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` and `spec/spec.md`.

P0 is the complete ritual and connected reading. P1 is safe share re-entry and resumable data tooling. P2 is provider-independent analytics plus accessibility and performance verification.

Resolved defaults: a normal refresh begins a fresh ritual; questions are never persisted; missing authored readings use deterministic fallback; a share token carries only version and ordered card ids; one optional, always-skippable affiliate suggestion appears after the reading pause.

Primary risks are interpretation quality, 78-card mobile performance, artwork provenance, affiliate trust, and question privacy. Each has an explicit acceptance check in `spec/spec.md`.
